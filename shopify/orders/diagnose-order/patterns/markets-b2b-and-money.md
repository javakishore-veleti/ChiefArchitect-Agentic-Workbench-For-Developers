# Markets, B2B and money

Resolve market, presentment currency, shop currency, purchasing entity, company location/catalog and tax/duty context independently. Country labels are not stable identifiers for market matching. Compare `shopMoney` and `presentmentMoney` from the same MoneyBag; do not add across currencies. B2B pricing or payment terms can differ even when product and customer appear identical.

For discount discrepancies, inspect both applications and line/shipping allocations, including post-edit current totals.
