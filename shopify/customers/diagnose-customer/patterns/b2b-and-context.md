# B2B and buyer context

Customer identity alone may not establish company, company-location, market, or catalog context. Capture customer ID, company/contact relationship, selected company location, country/market, and the context passed to Storefront queries. Never infer B2B eligibility from tags alone.

When pricing or product access differs, compare identical product/variant IDs with and without the intended buyer context. Route catalog or pricing mechanics to the product/storefront skill after proving the identity context.
