# Markets, B2B, and channel context

Record buyer country, presentment currency, market, sales channel, authenticated customer, company location, catalog, and price list used by the failing request. Repeat only with explicitly selected contexts. Eligibility and Function inputs may differ across online store, Hydrogen, checkout, and POS. Compare configuration identity and buyer context across environments; never assume all storefronts share a market or B2B setup. A pricing difference is not automatically a discount failure—separate catalog/market price formation from discount allocation.
