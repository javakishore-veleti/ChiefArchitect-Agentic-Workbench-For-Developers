# Combinations and discount classes

Inspect every candidate discount's `discountClasses` and `combinesWith` settings. Product, order, and shipping effects are distinct; a Function target or returned operation must align with the configured class. Determine whether Shopify rejected a requested code, selected a better candidate, or applied multiple allocations. Do not equate multiple entered codes with multiple applied discounts. For migrations to unified discount Functions, validate the current target and class model before interpreting legacy behavior.

Official: https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/build/discounts#discount-classes
