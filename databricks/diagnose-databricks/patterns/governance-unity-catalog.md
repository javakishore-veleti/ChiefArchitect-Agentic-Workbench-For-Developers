# Unity Catalog and governance

Resolve principal, groups, workspace binding, metastore, securable hierarchy, ownership, grants, row filters, column masks, storage credential, external location, and lineage. Compare effective privileges with the failing action; avoid broad grants as a diagnostic shortcut. Check account/workspace identity federation and catalog isolation. For cross-workspace access, verify binding and cloud IAM separately. Treat lineage and audit logs as evidence, not authorization. Recommend least privilege and time-bounded elevation with an approval trail.
